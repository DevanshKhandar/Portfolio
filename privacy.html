Overview

This website is a personal portfolio for Devansh Khandar, an electronics engineering student. It exists solely to showcase academic background, engineering projects, achievements, and contact channels. Your privacy is respected and protected.

What Data We Collect

None. This website does not collect, log, track, or store any personal data from its visitors.

  • No user accounts or sign-in systems
  • No web forms or comment fields
  • No database of visitors or user submissions
  • No analytics trackers or tracking scripts

When you choose to contact me using the email link provided on the site, the email is composed and sent directly from your personal email client. No message content is routed through or stored by this website.

Cookies and Local Storage

This website uses zero cookies. It does not place first-party cookies, third-party cookies, or tracking beacons in your browser. The only browser storage used is a single session flag (sessionStorage) that remembers whether you have already seen the intro animation; it never leaves your device, contains no personal data, and is cleared when you close the tab.

Third-Party Services

This website is intentionally minimal and keeps third-party dependencies to a minimum:

  • Google Fonts: The typography on this site (Space Grotesk, Inter, JetBrains Mono) is delivered via Google Fonts CDN. When your browser requests these font files, Google may receive your browser information and IP address for asset delivery purposes according to standard web protocols. No cookies are stored by Google Fonts.
  • External Profile Links: The website provides links to external profiles (LinkedIn, GitHub) and Google Drive (for certificate verification). Clicking these links navigates you to external platforms governed by their own privacy policies.
